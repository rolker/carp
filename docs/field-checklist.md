# Field Day Checklist

The bench phases live in `bringup-checklist.md`; this is the card for a dive
day. Print it, laminate it, clip it to the milk crate. (Reviews 1 minor 12 /
2 carry-forward.)

## The night before

- [ ] Packs charged (balance charge, in the pouches, supervised); per-cell
      voltages checked and logged before *and* after charge
- [ ] Pack inspection: any puffing, soft spots, or wrinkled pouch = that pack
      stays home
- [ ] Router power bank charged; phone charged; spare phone cable packed
- [ ] Vacuum check: pull 15 inHg on the sealed housing, note the reading —
      re-check at the launch. A soft vacuum overnight is a leak you found on
      land
- [ ] Desiccant indicating blue/orange (not pink/green); swap if saturated
- [ ] Field box inventory: spare XT90-S male, spare o-rings, o-ring grease,
      core tool, bike pump, zip ties, tape, towel, knife

## At the launch, before wetting the hull

- [ ] Vacuum still holding from last night
- [ ] XT90-S male pin inspected — pitting or a weak first-contact "tick"
      means swap the connector before diving (wiring.md rule 1)
- [ ] Pack connected, lid closed, **vent equalized then core seated**
      (ADR-013)
- [ ] Leak failsafe alive: `FS_LEAK = surface` confirmed in Cockpit
      parameters
- [ ] Tether flaked into the crate, no half-hitches; quick-release to the
      deck cleat — **never to your body**
- [ ] Downline dropped on the contact, clipped, surface float visible
- [ ] Router in the dry box, gland snug, lid latched; Cockpit connects;
      gamepad paired; phone on airplane-mode-plus-WiFi, battery saver off
- [ ] Control check at the surface: all axes, correct signs, disarm works

## In the water

- [ ] **Disarm before hands go anywhere near props** — every recovery, every
      weed clearing, every time
- [ ] Watch pack voltage, not the clock: ~39 min to 20% reserve at ~110 W is
      the model, the meter is the truth
- [ ] Snagged? The protocol is ADR-009, in order: gentle haul-test from
      multiple bearings → slack-and-drive → buoy the line and come back with
      help. **Never solo breath-hold dive on a fouled line**

## After

- [ ] Crack the vent core before opening the lid; a hiss is data (ADR-013)
- [ ] Paper-towel sweep inside the housing — damp means find it now, dry
      means log it
- [ ] Packs to storage charge (3.8 V/cell) before they go back in the pouches
- [ ] Rinse everything if the site was brackish; dry the tether before it
      coils
- [ ] Log the dive: runtime, pack mAh back in, vacuum readings, anything
      weird — while it's fresh
